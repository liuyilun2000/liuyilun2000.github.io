About Me

I am a PhD student in Computer Science at LMU Munich, supervised by Prof. Dr. Volker Tresp, where I study Language Models with a focus on their mechanistic interpretability, architectural designs, and post-training applications. Prior to this, I completed my M.Sc. in Data Engineering and Analytics at the Technical University of Munich, and my B.E. in Computer Science and Technology at Xi'an Jiaotong University.

arXiv preprint arXiv:2604.00801

Findings of the Association for Computational Linguistics: EACL 2026, 4439-4457
